About the workshop

Digital Twins connect robotic systems to the world they model. This workshop asks how to detect model–reality mismatch, understand uncertainty, and adapt safely in changing environments.

MODEL

Digital Twins & Physical AI

System integration and real-to-sim-to-real feedback.

DIAGNOSE

When Reality Differs

Mismatch detection, uncertainty and runtime monitoring.

ADAPT

Adaptation & Deployment

Model updates, failure recovery and human oversight.
