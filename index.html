About the workshop

Digital Twins connect robotic systems to the world they model. This workshop asks how to detect model–reality mismatch, understand uncertainty, and adapt safely in changing environments.

MODEL

Digital Twins & Physical AI

System integration and real-to-sim-to-real feedback.

DIAGNOSE

When Reality Differs

Mismatch detection, uncertainty and runtime monitoring.

ADAPT

Adaptation & Deployment

Model updates, failure recovery and human oversight.

Call for papers

Not yet open

We plan to welcome short papers and extended abstracts on Digital Twins, model–reality mismatch and trustworthy robotic deployment.

What to contribute

  • Research advances, datasets and benchmarks
  • Deployment case studies and practical demonstrations
  • Failure cases, negative results and lessons learned

How to participate

Peer-reviewed contributions will be presented as spotlights, posters or demonstrations. Submission format, deadlines and the submission link will be announced here.
