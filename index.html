THE CENTRAL QUESTION

When models meet
an imperfect world.

From simulation to deployment, trustworthy intelligence must stay connected to reality.

Digital Twins and physical world models offer powerful tools for simulation, system integration and runtime monitoring. Yet changes in geometry, materials, dynamics, sensing and operating environments can undermine the models that robotic systems depend on.

This workshop brings together researchers and practitioners in robotics, AI, automation and industry to study how systems can detect these discrepancies, understand their consequences and respond through adaptation, recovery or human intervention.

Our shared objective is to identify research challenges, establish evaluation priorities and develop a roadmap for trustworthy Physical AI throughout the system lifecycle.

SCIENTIFIC SCOPE

Three themes.
One continuous feedback loop.

Connecting models, evidence and action
across the deployment lifecycle.

01 / MODEL

Digital Twins
& Physical AI

Architectures that connect intelligent systems with their physical counterparts.

  • System architectures and integration
  • Physical world models
  • Real-to-sim-to-real feedback
02 / DIAGNOSE

When Reality
Differs

Recognizing when assumptions break and understanding why.

  • Model–reality mismatch detection
  • Uncertainty estimation
  • Runtime monitoring and diagnosis
03 / ADAPT

Adaptation
& Deployment

Turning evidence into reliable behavior in changing environments.

  • Model updates and failure recovery
  • Human oversight and safe operation
  • Manufacturing, manipulation and mobility
